Clever needs Google emails

In order for your students, teachers, and staff to log in with Google, their Google emails must be synced with Clever. Firstly, add Clever as trusted third-party app in Google admin console and choose the source of Google email addresses below. Learn more.

Set up in Google admin console
Add Clever as trusted third-party app

To use Clever with Google, the Google admin in your organization must add Clever as a trusted third-party app in the Google Admin Console. If this step is not completed, SSO will not work for all users.
